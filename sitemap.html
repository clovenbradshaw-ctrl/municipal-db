STR Registration & Oversight — visual site map

The point of the demo: show how a city could do STR registration and oversight — an applicant registers, staff oversee, and every claim drills down to the same evidence. Boots in demo mode (real Nashville parcel slice + dummy STR). Numbered gold cards = the 5-minute golden path.

Connect data backend

sidebar button · explicit action only
REAL · 15,693 events + geo-json

Fetches the full bridge corpus + geo layer, re-runs the same dummy-STR generator over all 2,489 parcels, flips the mode chip to backend. Every screen above re-renders unchanged — that sameness is the demo's argument. “Back to demo” returns to the slice.
